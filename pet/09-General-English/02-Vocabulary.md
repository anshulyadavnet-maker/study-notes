# 1. What is Vocabulary?

Vocabulary is the collection of words a learner understands and uses. In competitive exams, vocabulary questions test **meaning, word relationships, correct usage, spelling, and context**.

For PET, a useful vocabulary record contains:

1. Word
2. Meaning
3. Part of speech
4. Synonym
5. Antonym, where useful
6. Example sentence
7. Confusing word, where applicable
8. Correct spelling

::: tip
Do not memorise a word only as a Hindi translation. Learn it with a short English sentence so you can recognise it in a question or passage.
:::

# 8. Homophones and Confusing Words

| Word 1 | Word 2 | Difference |
|---|---|---|
| accept | except | receive/agree vs excluding |
| advice | advise | noun vs verb |
| affect | effect | usually verb vs usually noun |
| principal | principle | head/main vs rule/belief |
| stationery | stationary | writing materials vs not moving |
| complement | compliment | completes vs praise |
| loose | lose | not tight vs fail to keep |
| later | latter | afterwards vs second of two |
| beside | besides | next to vs in addition |
| personal | personnel | individual/private vs staff |
| adapt | adopt | adjust vs take up/accept |
| assure | ensure | tell confidently vs make certain |
| allusion | illusion | indirect reference vs false appearance |
| moral | morale | principle of right/wrong vs confidence/spirit |
| practice | practise | noun vs verb in British usage |
| conscience | conscious | moral sense vs aware |
| desert | dessert | dry region/abandon vs sweet course |

::: example
**Advice / Advise**

- He gave me useful **advice**. → noun
- I **advise** you to study. → verb

**Principal / Principle**

- The school **principal** addressed the students.
- Honesty is an important **principle**.
:::

# 10. Vocabulary in Context

When an unfamiliar word appears in a sentence or passage, use context clues.

## Definition Clue
The sentence directly explains the word.

## Contrast Clue
Words such as **but, however, unlike, although** may signal contrast.

## Example Clue
Examples around the word may reveal its meaning.

## Restatement Clue
A later phrase may express the same idea in simpler language.

::: strategy
**Context-solving method:**

1. Read the whole sentence.
2. Identify the likely part of speech.
3. Read before and after the word.
4. Look for definition, contrast, example, or restatement.
5. Substitute each option mentally.
6. Select the option that fits the complete context.
:::

# 13. Exam Vocabulary Strategy

## Before the Exam

- Learn 15–20 useful words regularly.
- Revise old words before adding new ones.
- Maintain synonym and antonym pairs.
- Learn one sentence for difficult words.
- Maintain a spelling-error list.
- Group confusing words together.

## During the Exam

1. Read the complete question.
2. Identify whether it asks synonym, antonym, usage, spelling, or substitution.
3. Eliminate clearly unrelated options.
4. Use context whenever available.
5. Do not spend excessive time on one unfamiliar word.

::: tip
**Best revision cycle:** Learn → use in a sentence → revise after 1 day → revise after 1 week → test yourself.
:::
