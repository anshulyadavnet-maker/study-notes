# General English — Vocabulary

> **PET Detailed Notes | English-first | StudyHub Point**
>
> **Learning sequence:** Meaning → Usage → Confusing pairs → Examples → Exam practice
>
> **Hindi Help:** Only where a meaning or distinction is difficult to understand from English alone.

---

# 1. What is Vocabulary?

Vocabulary is the collection of words a learner understands and uses. In competitive exams, vocabulary questions mainly test **meaning, word relationships, correct usage, spelling, and context**.

PET preparation should focus on both:

- **Word meaning** — What does the word mean?
- **Word usage** — How is the word correctly used in a sentence?

::: tip
**Exam Tip:** Do not memorise a synonym or antonym as an isolated pair. Learn the word with its **meaning + part of speech + one sentence + common opposite**.
:::

# 2. Synonyms

A **synonym** is a word that has the same or nearly the same meaning as another word in a particular context.

## Important Synonym Pairs

| Word | Synonym | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Abandon | Leave | give up/leave |
| Accurate | Exact | correct |
| Brave | Courageous | showing courage |
| Begin | Commence | start |
| Big | Large | great in size |
| Calm | Peaceful | free from disturbance |
| Difficult | Hard | not easy |
| Famous | Renowned | widely known |
| Honest | Truthful | not dishonest |
| Intelligent | Clever | mentally capable |
| Rapid | Fast | quick |
| Rare | Uncommon | not common |
| Reliable | Dependable | can be trusted |
| Select | Choose | pick |
| Vacant | Empty | unoccupied |

::: warning
Synonyms are often **context-dependent**. Two words may be similar in one sentence but not interchangeable in every situation.
:::

## Context Example

**Big** and **large** are often similar:

> a big house / a large house

But some fixed expressions prefer one form:

> a **big** mistake

The goal is not merely to replace words mechanically; understand their normal usage.

# 6. Idioms and Phrases

An **idiom** is a fixed expression whose overall meaning may not be predictable from the individual words.

## Important Idioms

| Idiom | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A blessing in disguise | something that seems bad but turns out beneficial | The delay was a blessing in disguise. |
| A piece of cake | very easy | The test was a piece of cake. |
| At the eleventh hour | at the last moment | He submitted the form at the eleventh hour. |
| Break the ice | start a friendly interaction | The teacher told a joke to break the ice. |
| Hit the nail on the head | say/do exactly the right thing | You hit the nail on the head. |
| Once in a blue moon | very rarely | He visits us once in a blue moon. |
| Spill the beans | reveal a secret | She spilled the beans. |
| Under the weather | feeling unwell | I am feeling under the weather. |
| Call it a day | stop working for the day | Let's call it a day. |
| In hot water | in trouble | He is in hot water. |

::: warning
Do not translate an idiom word-for-word into Hindi. Learn its **complete meaning as a unit**.

**Hindi Help:** Idiom का अर्थ अक्सर literal meaning से अलग होता है।
:::

# 8. Homophones and Confusing Words

Homophones may sound alike but differ in spelling and meaning.

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| accept | receive/agree | He accepted the offer. |
| except | excluding | Everyone came except Ravi. |
| affect | usually verb | Noise affects health. |
| effect | usually noun | The effect was clear. |
| advice | noun | He gave me advice. |
| advise | verb | I advise you to study. |
| principal | head/main | The principal addressed the students. |
| principle | rule/belief | It is a basic principle. |
| stationery | writing materials | Buy some stationery. |
| stationary | not moving | The car remained stationary. |
| complement | something that completes | The tie complements the shirt. |
| compliment | praise | She gave me a compliment. |

::: trap
**Advice** is a noun; **advise** is a verb.  
**Principal** can mean a school head or something main; **principle** means a rule/belief.
:::

| re- | again | rewrite |

## Common Suffixes

| Suffix | Common function | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -ness | noun | kindness |

# 10. Vocabulary in Context

When an unfamiliar word appears in a sentence or passage, use context clues.

## Four Useful Clues

### 1. Definition clue
The sentence directly explains the word.

### 2. Contrast clue
Words such as **but, however, unlike, although** may signal an opposite or contrast.

### 3. Example clue
The following examples may explain the unknown word.

### 4. Restatement clue
A second phrase may repeat the meaning in simpler language.

::: strategy
**Context Strategy:**

1. Read the complete sentence.
2. Identify the part of speech.
3. Look for contrast/example/definition clues.
4. Substitute each option mentally.
5. Choose the meaning that fits the whole context.
:::

# 11. Synonym vs Antonym — Quick Difference

| Synonym | Antonym |
|---|---|
| Similar meaning | Opposite/contrasting meaning |
| happy → joyful | happy → sad |
| begin → start | begin → end |
| accurate → correct | accurate → inaccurate |

# 13. Exam Vocabulary Strategy

## Before the Exam

- Learn 15–20 useful words daily.
- Revise old words before adding new ones.
- Maintain synonym + antonym pairs.
- Learn one sentence for difficult words.
- Maintain a personal spelling-error list.

## During the Exam

- Read the complete question.
- Identify whether it asks synonym, antonym, usage, spelling, or substitution.
- Eliminate clearly unrelated options.
- Use context when available.
- Do not spend excessive time on one unfamiliar word.

::: tip
**Best revision cycle:** Learn → use in a sentence → revise after 1 day → revise after 1 week → test yourself.
:::
